🎉 Now you have a great way to take AI meeting minutes

Record tab audio from Google Meet or other calls, get a live transcript, and a summary — all locally in your browser.

1.

Pin the extension for quick access to AI Meeting Minutes.

How to pin

Open the puzzle icon in the Chrome toolbar → find AI Meeting Minutes → click Pin. Replace images/how-to-pin.svg with a screenshot (PNG or WebP) for a photo like on mp4-to-mp3.pro/welcome.

2.

Click the extension icon on your meeting tab to open the side panel.

How to open

Go to the tab where the call plays (e.g. Meet in Chrome) → click the AI Meeting Minutes icon → press Start in the side panel. You can swap images/how-to-open.svg for your own screenshot.
